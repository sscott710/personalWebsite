# About Me
I am a PhD student in History at George Mason University. As a graduate research assistant at the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media, I do web development and data visualization for data-driven and digital public history projects. My broad research interests are 20th century United States, war and society, and digital history. I am currently researching the development of the Army Outdoor Recreation Program, its relation to the transition to the All-Volunteer Force, and American environmental culture.

I am originally from Salisbury, MD, and received my BA in History from Bridgewater College. When not doing history, I enjoy golfing, reading (fiction!), and live music.
